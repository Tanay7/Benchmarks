# 05 - Telemetry & Command Dictionary

> GENERATED by `tools/gen_dictionary_doc.py` from `dssq-groundstation/python/dssq/dictionary.py` and the flight source. Do not edit by hand; re-run the script. The flight packers and the command parser are verified against the same dictionary by `tests/test_cross_check.py` (bit-exact).

Conventions: all fields big-endian (CCSDS network order). Engineering value = raw x scale, or the named decoder applied to the raw code. Not-available sentinels (column N/A, decoded as `None` and shown as `-`): i16 0x7FFF, u16 0xFFFF, i32 0x7FFFFFFF, u32 0xFFFFFFFF, i8 -128; `reading` marks saturating counters, bit masks and time fields, where every raw value is a reading. Every TM packet carries a 6-octet CUC secondary header (SCLK, CCSDS 301.0-B-4, 4 + 2 octets) before the user data listed here.

## 1 Telemetry packets

### Variable-length packets

| APID | Name | VC | Layout |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0x012 | EVR | 0 | `severity u8 \| event_id u16 \| ASCII text (<= 80 characters)`; the ground shows octets outside 0x20..0x7E as U+FFFD |
| 0x015 | RFSCAN | 0 | `first_ch u8 \| count u8 (1..84) \| duration_ms u16 \| noise_dbm i8 x count` (-128 = no reading) |
| 0x024 | SPECBURST | 1 | `t0_ms u32 \| rate_dhz u16 \| gain_code u8 \| int_cycles u8 \| n u8 (1..3) \| encoding u8 \| dt_ms u16[n] \| payload`; encoding 0: n x 18 u16 counts, time-major; 1: CCSDS 121.0-B-3 (16-bit samples, J = 16, r = 128, Basic, unit-delay preprocessor) of the channel-major counts, strictly shorter than raw. Header 10 octets, at most 124. Count 0xFFFF = not converted, 0xFFFE = saturated (>= 65534) |
| 0x030 | CFDP | 3 | one CCSDS 727.0-B-5 class-1 PDU of 10..146 octets (Metadata, File Data, EOF); never recorded in the SSR |

## 2 Packet generation cadence per flight-software mode

Seconds between packets (overridable per packet with `PKTRATE`). The spacecraft stretches every period at run time so that the periodic load stays within 75 % of the measured downlink capacity (EVR 0x0215 reports the factor). EVR, CMDVER, RFSCAN, SPECBURST and CFDP are event driven.

| Mode | HK | RF | TIMECORR | MAG | ATT | SPEC | ENV |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| BOOT | 10 s | 10 s | off | off | off | off | off |
| SAFE | 30 s | 60 s | 300 s | off | off | off | off |
| CRUISE | 60 s | 60 s | 300 s | 30 s | 60 s | 300 s | 120 s |
| ENCOUNTER | 20 s | 30 s | 120 s | 4 s | 8 s | 30 s | 30 s |
| TEST | 10 s | 10 s | 60 s | 4 s | 8 s | 30 s | 30 s |

## 4 Telecommands (uplinked; APID 0x0C0, user data = opcode + arguments)
